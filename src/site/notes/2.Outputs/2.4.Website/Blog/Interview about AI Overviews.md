I got the chance to appear on the **[The DMA Agora podcast](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/ai-overviews-what-now-alba-ribera-mart%C3%ADnez-ekgge/)** hosted by [Alba Ribera Martínez](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alba-ribera-martinez/) where we discussed Google's AI Overview feature in Google Search. Among other topics, we covered:

- How AI Overview alters the flow of traffic around the internet.
- The various harms of AI Overview: its distortion of competition for traffic, the lack of choice offered to websites, Google's unfair advantage in AI training, and harm to consumers' choice.
- Why regulators are focusing on publishers' lack of choice, rather than harms to competition.
- What regulators might do about AI Overview.
